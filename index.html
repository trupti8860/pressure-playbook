TRANSPARENCY / SCORING FRAMEWORK

THE METHOD.

How the pressure-performance shortlist was constructed.

03 SCORE FACTORS
01—

DEFINE THE PRESSURE.

Deliveries were classified using innings context, required run rate, wickets remaining and overs remaining. Revised innings lengths and targets were used where supplied.

01 / SECOND INNINGS

CHASE INTENSITY

Required run rate of at least 10, or four or fewer wickets remaining with 60 or fewer balls left, or the final 12 balls with runs still needed.

02 / FIRST INNINGS

LATE-INNINGS PRESSURE

Final 30 balls with at least five wickets down, or the final 12 balls of the innings.

02—

MEASURE PERFORMANCE.

Each player receives a composite score from three percentile-ranked metrics within their discipline. Higher composite score indicates stronger relative performance across the chosen factors.

A
BATTER FACTOR 01

PRESSURE STRIKE RATE

Higher pressure strike rate receives a higher percentile score.

B
BATTER FACTOR 02

DISMISSAL RATE

Lower pressure dismissal rate receives a higher percentile score.

C
BATTER FACTOR 03

STRIKE-RATE UPLIFT

Higher pressure strike rate relative to normal-phase strike rate receives a higher score.

D
BOWLER FACTOR 01

PRESSURE ECONOMY

Lower pressure economy receives a higher percentile score.

E
BOWLER FACTOR 02

WICKET RATE

More wickets per 100 pressure legal balls receives a higher score.

F
BOWLER FACTOR 03

ECONOMY IMPROVEMENT

Greater improvement from normal economy to pressure economy receives a higher score.

03—

APPLY ELIGIBILITY.

Minimum sample sizes help reduce the influence of very small samples. Players below either threshold remain visible as provisional candidates, without a fabricated rank.

BATTER THRESHOLD60 + 120

At least 60 pressure balls and 120 normal balls faced.

BOWLER THRESHOLD90 + 180

At least 90 pressure legal balls and 180 normal legal balls.

!

READ THE DATA RESPONSIBLY.

This is a historical IPL pressure-performance shortlist, not a guarantee of future performance or auction availability. A provisional label means the player does not meet the stated minimum sample size. Player selection also involves role fit, team balance, budget and auction conditions, which are not modelled by this pressure score.
